# Contributing

This project is **free to use** (Apache-2.0) but runs under a **single-maintainer**
model, by design.

- **Bug reports and ideas:** very welcome — please open an issue.
- **Pull requests:** considered at the maintainer's **sole discretion**, and may
  be declined without a detailed rationale. Please open an issue to discuss
  *before* investing time in a PR.
- **No expectation of merge.** The canonical codebase is intentionally kept under
  tight control to protect its architecture and stability. You are of course free
  to fork under the Apache-2.0 license.

Think of it like SQLite's model: broadly free to use, deliberately closed to
outside code in the canonical repository.
